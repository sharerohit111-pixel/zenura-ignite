07 / AFTER-LAUNCH CARE

We build it.
We maintain it.

ZENURA IGNITE also provides maintenance and technical support for apps, websites and software developed by us. We will take responsibility for carrying out the agreed maintenance work; maintenance is a separate paid service and is not automatically included in development or purchase charges.

OPTION 01
↻

Annual Maintenance Fee (AMC)

Pay an agreed maintenance fee once every year for the maintenance coverage defined in your service agreement.

  • Routine health checks and agreed updates
  • Covered bug fixes and technical assistance within the agreed scope
  • Coverage, response time and limits written in advance
OPTION 02
⚙

Per-Issue / Per-Repair Charges

If you do not choose an annual plan, you can request maintenance whenever an issue occurs. We will inspect the issue, share the estimated charge and begin repair after your approval.

  • Charges depend on the type and complexity of the issue
  • Estimate shared before chargeable repair work
  • No repair charge without your approval

Maintenance fees are separate from app/software development, one-time purchase or annual rental charges. New features, major changes, third-party service fees, hosting/domain renewals and work outside an AMC's agreed coverage are quoted separately. Exact pricing and service scope will be confirmed in writing before work begins.
