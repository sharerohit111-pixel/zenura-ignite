Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

1. About this policy

This policy explains how ZENURA IGNITE TECHNOLOGIES handles information when you browse this website, create an account, or contact us about a project.

2. Information you provide

Depending on the features you use, information may include your name, email address or phone number, account profile details, and project enquiry details such as service interest, budget range and message.

3. How information is handled

Where Firebase account and database features are enabled, sign-in and related profile or enquiry records are handled through the configured Firebase services. If online enquiry storage is unavailable, the enquiry form prepares an email in your device's email app; you choose whether to send it. Do not include passwords, payment card details or other sensitive information in an enquiry.

4. Purpose and sharing

Information is used to provide requested website features, respond to enquiries, manage customer accounts and improve our services. We do not sell personal information. Service providers needed to operate enabled website features may process information on our behalf under their own terms and privacy practices.

5. Your choices and contact

You may choose what information to submit and can contact us to request access, correction or deletion where applicable. Email: zenuraignite@gmail.com. We may need to verify your request before acting on it.

6. Updates

We may update this policy as website features change. The latest version and its update date will be published on this page.
