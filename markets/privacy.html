Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ZENURA IGNITE Technologies ("ZENURA IGNITE", "we", "us") handles information when you use the ZENURA IGNITE S-TRADE Indian-market paper-trading application ("Service"). The Service is a paper-trading simulation. It does not place real-money exchange orders.

Please read this policy before creating an account or connecting a third-party account.

1. Information we process

  • Account and sign-in information: email address and authentication identifiers. If you choose Google sign-in, Google may provide basic profile information such as your name, email address and profile image, subject to the permissions shown on Google's consent screen.
  • Paper-trading information: information you create or use in the Service, which may include watchlists, simulated orders, positions, holdings, virtual account balances, trade history and related account preferences.
  • Optional broker connection: if you choose to connect Upstox, the authorization and account information needed to provide the requested connection features may be processed. Do not connect an account unless you understand and accept the permissions shown by the provider.
  • Technical information: session and security data needed for sign-in, session continuity, fraud prevention, troubleshooting and operation of the Service. Your browser may store authentication session information and cache public application files.
  • Support messages: information you choose to send when contacting us.

2. How we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts, authenticate users, maintain sessions, provide paper-trading features, save user-requested workspace data, support optional provider connections, prevent abuse, troubleshoot errors and respond to support requests.

3. Service providers and sharing

We use third-party services to operate features you request. Supabase provides authentication and application backend services. Google provides Google sign-in when you choose it. Upstox may process information when you separately authorize an Upstox connection. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. We do not sell personal information. We do not share information with third parties for their independent advertising purposes.

We may disclose information if required by law, to protect users or the Service, or in connection with a legitimate business reorganization, subject to applicable law.

4. Market data and simulated trading

S-TRADE is designed for paper trading. Orders, balances, positions and profit/loss shown as paper-trading records are simulated and are not instructions to a broker or an exchange. If an optional market-data or broker connection is enabled, the relevant provider may process requests and data under its own terms. Availability and display of market data are subject to provider permissions and applicable exchange rules.

5. Storage, security and retention

Account and application information is processed using the Service's configured backend providers. We use access controls and security measures intended to protect information, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Information is retained for as long as needed to provide the Service, meet legitimate operational needs, resolve disputes and comply with legal obligations. Retention periods may depend on the relevant provider and account configuration.

6. Your choices and requests

You can choose whether to use Google sign-in or email/password sign-in, and whether to authorize an optional Upstox connection. You may stop using the Service and contact us to request access, correction or deletion of account-related information. Some records may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate security and operational purposes. You can also review Google's and Supabase's privacy controls through their respective services.

7. Children's privacy

The Service is not intended for children under 18. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children under 18. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review the request.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Service changes. The updated version will be published at this page with a revised effective date. Continued use after an update means the updated policy applies to future use, subject to applicable law.
